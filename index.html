Our Story

From Homemade Biscuits to Thoughtful Treat-Making

I started Nourish & Nibble by making ordinary homemade biscuits with care in my own kitchen. Over time, I pursued qualifications and developed a deeper interest in supporting canine health and wellbeing through thoughtful treat-making.

Today, every pouch is prepared mindfully in South Africa using familiar, wholesome ingredients—starting with Happy Gut Bites made from chicken, pumpkin, sweet potato and rolled oats.
